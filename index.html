Security Policy

1. Account Security
All BitVault Pro accounts are protected by password hashing (bcrypt), two-factor authentication (2FA), and email verification. We strongly recommend enabling 2FA on all accounts.

The majority of client digital assets are held in offline cold storage wallets with multi-signature authorization. Hot wallets maintain only the minimum operational balance required for daily withdrawals.

3. Encryption Standards
All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256. API communications require signed requests with HMAC-SHA256 authentication.

BitVault Pro employs 24/7 automated monitoring for suspicious login attempts, unusual withdrawal patterns, and anomalous trading activity. Accounts flagged by our systems may be temporarily frozen for manual review.
